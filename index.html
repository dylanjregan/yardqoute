How to calculate landscaping materials

Square feet

Multiply length by width in feet. Add a trimming allowance for sod and pavers.

Cubic yards

Multiply length and width in feet by depth in inches, then divide by 324. Add extra for waste where appropriate. Ask suppliers about compaction and settling.

Water volume

One inch of water over one square foot is approximately 0.623 gallons. Adjust irrigation for weather, soil and plant needs.

Ordering materials

Measure each area separately, check units, and round up to supplier packaging. These tools are not structural or irrigation design advice.
